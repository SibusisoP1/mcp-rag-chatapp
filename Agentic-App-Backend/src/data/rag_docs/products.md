## 1. LAPTOP DIVISION (Model: StreamBook 15)

- **Standard Price:** $100.00 (Refurbished/Education Edition) / $1,000.00 (Pro Flagship Edition)
- **Display:** 15.6-inch Full HD LED-backlit screen.
- **Operating System:** StreamOS Pre-installed.
- **Box Contents:** StreamBook 15 Laptop, 65W USB-C Power Adapter, and Quick Start Guide.

### 1.1 Power and Battery Troubleshooting

- **Battery Life:** Up to 8 hours of continuous video playback.
- **Hard Reset Sequence:** If the laptop screen freezes or refuses to power on, press and hold the physical power button for exactly 10 seconds. Release, wait 5 seconds, and press it once more to boot.

## 2. SMARTPHONE DIVISION (Model: NexusPhone Lite)

- **Standard Price:** $50.00
- **Display:** 6.1-inch OLED adaptive display.
- **Connectivity:** 5G Enabled, Bluetooth 5.2, and Wi-Fi 6.

### 2.1 SIM Card & Activation Steps

1. Locate the SIM tray on the bottom left edge of the phone.
2. Insert the standard SIM ejector tool into the pinhole to pop open the tray.
3. Place your Nano-SIM card face down into the tray slot.
4. Push the tray firmly back into the smartphone until it clicks flush with the frame.

## 3. TABLET DIVISION (Model: ViewPad 10)

- **Standard Price:** $200.00
- **Display:** 10.2-inch Liquid Retina touch display.
- **Storage Capacity:** 64GB Base Model (Expandable up to 256GB via MicroSD).

### 3.1 Factory Reset Workflow (Data Wipe)

- **Warning:** A factory reset will permanently erase all data, apps, and photos from the ViewPad 10.
- **Execution Steps:**
  1. Open the _Settings App_ on the tablet.
  2. Scroll down and tap **System & Updates**.
  3. Select **Reset Options** and click **Erase All Data (Factory Reset)**.
  4. Type your 4-digit device PIN code to confirm the erase block sequence.

## 4. SYSTEM MAINTENANCE & ENVIRONMENT RESTRICTIONS

- **Water Damage Policy:** None of the standard devices (Laptop, Smartphone, or Tablet) are completely waterproof. Liquid exposure instantly voids the product warranty.
- **Charging Safety:** Always use the official charging bricks included in the box. Using uncertified third-party charging cables can cause battery swelling or permanent motherboard damage.
